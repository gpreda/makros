Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Makros, we collect the following information:

  • Google account data: your email address, name, and Google user ID (used for authentication)
  • Fitbit data: body weight measurements (if you connect your Fitbit account)
  • User-entered data: meal logs, food items, daily weight entries, exercise entries, caloric targets, and progress photos/videos

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Service to you — displaying nutrition summaries, weight trends, and progress tracking. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database on our server. We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Data is only shared with:

  • Google: for authentication (OAuth sign-in)
  • Fitbit: to retrieve your weight data (only if you connect your Fitbit account)

5. Coach Feature

If you add a coach, that coach will be able to view your nutrition logs, weight data, and caloric targets. Coaches cannot modify your data except for caloric targets.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. If you wish to have your data deleted, please contact the site administrator.

7. Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking cookies or analytics cookies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to request data deletion, please contact the site administrator.
